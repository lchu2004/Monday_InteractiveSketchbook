Save Me An Orange

Written by Hayley Grace



Chapter 3 / Oranges

Page 87

i hope one day you find me in a grocery store
i'll have new tattoos
but my perfume will stay the same
our eyes will lock
while i stand beside an aisle of fruit
your heart sinks to the ground
and i'll lose my voice
all over again


we will laught about how long it's been
and you will apologize
for your immaturities


i'll wish you well
and before you're on your way
i'll remind you to not feel guilty
because after all
we were only 15.
