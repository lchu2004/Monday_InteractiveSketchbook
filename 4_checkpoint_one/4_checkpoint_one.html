Process Reflection

When a visitor encounters my page, I want them to see my work immediately. This way, it will give them a better look at my professional work and my previous work. This way, the visitor can get ideas and also contact me if they want a certain artwork done that I have done previously. Information that belongs on the entry page would be social media, about me, and a good way to contact the owner or company. Something we can also put could be reviews from either Google or Yelp! I want someone to move through my pages through hyperlinks or scroll down and click on the section they need. Since I plan to do photography and graphic design, they can choose which service they would like. Something that should remain consistent is the navigation, header, and socials/contact us down in the header. This way, visitors can always head back to the home page or contact us when they are ready to start a new project with me. As the site gets larger, I think I will have to change the navigation page to sort all of my pages later on. Knowing I have a lot of pages to fill in.
