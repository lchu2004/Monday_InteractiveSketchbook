Process Reflection

This was the hardest assignment you could have given me. I genuily hate this assignment but it did teach me a lot. I sweat a little bit and my brain hurts. Never again should you give me this hard of a CSS project. The color and the spacing was the worst part. I learned a lot about spacing and displays though which was good. Would I want to do this again? NO. Make it a little easier and then I will consider. I had to do numerous spacings and reload the page to even find the perfect spacing. Marging did help a lot but it got to the point where I had to use another code which was gap and display. I love the grouping of these codes tho!
