Process Reflection

This assignment was very confusing to me; I had to contact a classmate for some help. Divs are still a little confusing to me, but I am getting a better understanding! I think the hardest part for me still is the margins and the placement of wording. A lot of testing is needed to find the exact position I am satisfied with. The width was also very confusing because it affected a lot of codes at the same time. I put a div class on everything to make the whole project a flex, and then I started messing around with the margins and padding. I think the whole flexbox is still confusing until I get better at the padding and width along with margin. I usually put it in auto but it was not satisfying me.
